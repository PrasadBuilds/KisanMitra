# KisanMitra — Day 27 Evaluation Report

## Objective
Evaluate the trained CNN model for tomato leaf disease classification.

## Dataset
- Total images: 4,500
- Training images: 3,600
- Validation images: 900
- Classes: Early Blight, Healthy, Late Blight

## Results
- Best validation accuracy: 78.11%
- Validation loss: 0.4736
- Best model: `kisanmitra_tomato_cnn_best.keras`

## Class-wise F1 Scores
- Early Blight: 0.57
- Healthy: 0.89
- Late Blight: 0.77

## Conclusion
The model achieved 78.11% validation accuracy. Healthy leaf classification performed well, but Early Blight detection needs improvement. Further testing is required before practical use.

## Next Step
Investigate classification errors and improve the model.
